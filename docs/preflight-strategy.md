# Product-image preflight: strategy and distribution

Decision proposal, 2026-09-08. Source baseline and sequencing:
[roadmap](../ROADMAP.md) and [execution ledger](workbench-execution-plan.md).
No market-size, conversion-rate or competitor-superiority claim is made here.

## Position the outcome, not the codec list

The durable product hypothesis is **a local batch arrives; usable deliverables
and an intelligible account of the work leave**. Encoding, models and presets
support that outcome. Preflight means checking a job before delivery, not granting
marketplace approval or certifying rights and accurate product depiction.

Suggested future headline: **Prepare, verify and deliver product images.**
Until the relevant features are verified, describe the actual local offering:
audit, prepare and inspect images locally, with optional Studio actions. Explain
which delivery/checking features are planned. Preserve the fast one-folder path;
product grouping must remain optional.

Use one clear relationship wherever the products meet: ImageGuide identifies
problems; Press prepares files; Studio manages shared business work. Press should
have a recognizable product/download destination rather than making visitors
infer this relationship from three repositories. A landing-page change is a
separate site PR, not functionality delivered by this documentation change.

## Acquisition and retention are different questions

| Cohort | Acquisition hypothesis | Retention hypothesis | Evidence that would change the plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retailer-invited supplier | A real assignment makes the tool relevant immediately | Repeated submissions and corrections justify keeping it | Installation/support costs exceed the benefit for occasional jobs; favor the browser |
| Photographer, agency or ecommerce content operator | A useful local batch workflow earns a trial | The same masters repeatedly need different destinations and specs | Jobs are mostly one-off, or existing export tooling already solves them |
| Site owner or developer using ImageGuide | An observed problem creates demand for remediation | Repeated asset preparation or project reuse | Local originals cannot be mapped reliably, or the bottleneck is deployment/markup |
| Agent/CLI user | Safe local automation removes repetitive file work | Saved recipes, reports and repeatable project tasks become part of a workflow | Installation or contract instability outweighs the convenience |

These cohorts are tests, not settled personas. The invited supplier may generate
retailer value without becoming a daily active user or buying a personal plan.
An agency may become a frequent user without ever needing Supplier Portal.
Do not judge both against the same subscription conversion or daily-use target.

## Make the loops real

**Diagnosis to remediation.** A user exports selected ImageGuide findings, opens
that report in Press, confirms matching originals, prepares a batch, deploys the
files through their existing site workflow and re-audits. Local export does not
close a website finding. The [handoff plan](browser-handoff-plan.md) keeps image,
markup and delivery work separate, and preserves measured versus estimated facts.

**One master to repeat business work.** Start with two user-defined targets, not
hundreds of marketplace presets. A retained master copy plus a website derivative
is sufficient to test repeat value. Independent outputs, changed-source detection
and retrying one failed target should save repeated setup. The
[recipe plan](delivery-recipes.md) specifies that first vertical slice.

**Agent intent to accountable local work.** The agent interprets the request;
Press resolves supported operations, executes only authorized writes and reports
actual outcomes. Keep the CLI first. A protocol wrapper is not a substitute for
stable semantics. See the [agent plan](agent-execution-plan.md).

**Supplier request to accepted work.** Studio resolves the recipient's requirements;
Press helps prepare and submit the exact reviewed files; the normal review and
correction loop remains authoritative. Continue the existing supplier plan rather
than creating a parallel folder-upload path.

## Requirements and evidence as reusable value

A reusable, versioned requirement pack can be useful to a person, an agent and a
Studio supplier task. It identifies the target and what can be checked; a recipe
identifies transforms; a receipt identifies the actual output and evidence.
This is a potential differentiator, not an established standard or network effect.
Start as an internal interoperable contract with fixtures. Do not build a standards
organization, public registry or policy marketplace to prove one retailer's job.

Local receipts are explanatory evidence, not tamper-proof certificates. Studio
revalidates submitted bytes and owns approval. Improvements in repeatable checks,
maintained target knowledge and workflow integration matter more here than counting
additional image operations.

## Channel order and experiments

Use existing surfaces before buying broad acquisition: retailer task invitations,
ImageGuide's relevant tool pages and extension, then agent/CLI examples and useful
operator referrals. Keep installation optional and return users to their original
task. A shareable preset/report must be useful without a lead-capture form.

Run small observational pilots, not a manufactured statistically significant test.
Recruit roughly five to ten willing participants per investigated cohort only when
practical; reuse the retailer pilot in [packaging](adoption-and-packaging.md).
Agree the job, comparison process and expansion threshold before observing results.
Record why a user did not complete a step, not just that a click occurred.

| Experiment | Primary evidence and denominator | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Browser handoff | Completed, correctly mapped local remediation jobs / attempted handoffs; separately, re-audits after deployment | Improve source mapping or deployment guidance before adding native launch machinery |
| Two-target delivery | Repeat real jobs / activated operators who had another eligible job; active preparation time versus their prior method | Expand target/check coverage only where it removes repeated work |
| Agent workflow | Correctly completed authorized tasks / attempted tasks; misleading reports, unsafe writes and setup failures reported separately | Fix the CLI contract before promoting more wrappers |
| Supplier pilot | Reviewable submissions without live help / attempted jobs; correction rounds and support minutes | Expand when retailer value survives real setup and recovery costs |

Downloads and browser visits are diagnostic, not the goal. Use consented observation
for offline work and necessary server receipts for connected work. Do not collect
paths, filenames, SKUs, prompts, authenticated URLs or client lists as product
analytics. No mandatory local telemetry; no signup required to export personal
presets or ordinary local reports.

The commercial boundary remains the [packaging proposal](adoption-and-packaging.md):
complete local utility, paid hosted/persistent/shared service value. Measure service
margin after retries, storage/transfer, refunds and support; do not assume credit
consumption is success. Retailer sponsorship is a later explicit budgeted service.

## Investment rule

First fix the concrete obstacle to a real job. HEIC ingress may outrank another
model when a participant cannot open their folder. Mapping may outrank one-click
launch when a report cannot identify originals. Supplier access may outrank every
organic acquisition idea when a retailer pilot is ready.

Press can remain an enjoyable, useful independent tool while these hypotheses are
tested. This plan does not impose a full startup roadmap, simultaneous workstreams
or an obligation to monetize every feature. It identifies the next useful options
and the evidence needed to choose between them.
